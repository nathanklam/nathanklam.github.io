Side Projects

Personal CAD & Replacement Parts

Outside of coursework and combat robotics, I also use CAD for everyday problems and personal projects: designing replacement parts for things that broke and modeling ideas just for fun. These are smaller one-offs, but each one still meant measuring, fitting, and iterating until the part worked.

Air Conditioner Knob Replacement (2026)

Replacement part · Calipers, CAD, 3D printing

Problem: A friend's air conditioning unit had a broken control knob, and the part (4941AR7134E) is no longer sold.

Approach: Working from the broken piece they gave me, I measured each feature with calipers and remodeled the knob in CAD so it could be 3D printed.

Result: A printable replacement that restores the unit's controls without needing the discontinued original.

Caduceus Costume Prop (2026)

For fun · CAD, 3D printing

Goal: A costume prop based on the Caduceus from a video game, built around a single grip with a bayonet mount so the same grip can lock onto several different weapon versions.

Modeling: Using Onshape, I worked from in-game reference images, scaling them to real-world proportions and tracing the profiles with splines. The organic shapes pushed me into features I don't normally reach for, especially lofts.

Design for assembly: Every part is 3D printed, so each section has to fit within the printer's height limit. The main design goal is that the prop splits cleanly into printable sections that are easy to assemble and take apart again, for both printing and transport.
