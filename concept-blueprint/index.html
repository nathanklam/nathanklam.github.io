DWG 002

“Skadoosh” (Winter 2026-), “Eggsterminator” (Fall 2024)

Eggsterminator was a 1 lb plastic combat robot focused on survivability through angled geometry and print-oriented design, earning Most Destructive in an internal competition. Skadoosh builds on this foundation with an improved weapon system and experimental front-wedge geometry to add controlled engagement and opponent manipulation capabilities. The second iteration went back to conventional design, winning several matches. The third iteration is in assembly.

Tools/Skills: Fusion 360 · 3D Printing · Soldering · Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing

Coursework Projects

DWG 005

Culinary Bot (Spring 2026)

This project presents a robotic culinary plating assistant that uses a UR7e arm with an attached camera to capture a completed dish from multiple viewpoints and reconstruct a 3D model of the plated arrangement. The system then identifies food items, estimates their poses relative to varying frames, and generates an accurate pick-and-place plan to recreate the dish.

Course: MEC106A - Introduction to Robotics

Tools/Skills: ROS2 · YOLO · Python · HTML · CSS

DWG 006

Composite Armor in Combat Robotics Applications (Spring 2026)

A simulation project, investigating the behavior of composites under medium-velocity impacts (up to 110 m/s) with non-linear trajectories and high mass. The goal is to explore and possibly develop alternatives to aluminum 6061 and steel for armor applications in combat robotics.

Course: ME127 - Introduction to Composite Materials

Tools/Skills: Ansys · LS-DYNA · Fusion 360 · LaTeX
